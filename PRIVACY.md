# QA Toolkit privacy notice

Updated: 2026-10-15.

QA Toolkit is a package of instructions, templates, and reference files for
software testing. It has no publisher-operated backend, bundled tracker
connection, analytics, or telemetry. Installing the package does not send
your testing inputs to Alexander Danvall, its publisher.

## Information you provide

The workflows use the information you choose to provide: requirements,
test cases, field definitions, test results, defect descriptions, screenshots,
logs, and tool exports. These may contain internal project information,
personal data, or customer data. The information is used to create or review
the testing output you requested.

Your AI assistant processes messages, attachments, and generated output
under that assistant provider's terms, privacy policy, and account settings.
If you authorize a connected integration to read from or publish to a
tracker or another service, that service also receives the relevant data
under its own policies. QA Toolkit does not supply these integrations.

## Sensitive information and your choices

The skills default to masking sensitive values in their output. Bug Report
also supports explicit, destination-specific Preserve and Include all
choices, made in the conversation or in team conventions. Preserve keeps
needed personal or customer details and masks credentials. Include all can
keep supplied credentials in the report. Team conventions may forbid these
modes. Output masking does not remove
information already supplied to your assistant.

Review the draft and destination before sharing it. The skills instruct the
assistant to request confirmation before filing or posting; these are model
instructions, not an access-control system.

## Storage and retention

The package provides no publisher-managed storage or retention period for
your messages, attachments, or reports. Their retention and deletion depend
on your assistant, connected services, local files, and organization settings.
Use those products' controls to manage or delete them.

Team conventions are stored where your team keeps them: usually in
`.qa-toolkit/conventions.md` in your project, otherwise in conventions you
supply in the conversation or workspace, or in each skill's
`references/team-conventions.md` in a copy you maintain. The skills read
these files; Team Setup, and a skill whose offer you accept, write
`.qa-toolkit/conventions.md` only after you approve the draft. They never
commit or share it. The instructions prohibit putting credentials or real
customer data in it, because the file is meant to be shared with your team.

## Support

Information you voluntarily post in a public
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/alexpalexpinne/qa-toolkit/issues) is visible
to others and retained by GitHub. Use fictional reproductions and remove
private information before posting. For a privacy question, open an issue
without including the private information in question.
